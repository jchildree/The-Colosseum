Privacy Policy - The Colosseum

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Overview

The Colosseum is a desktop application that helps administrators configure, draft, and publish custom video game tournaments. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored, and how it is used.

Information We Collect

The app collects only information you enter directly:

  • Google Forms access: with your permission, the app uses your Google account to access the one signup form you choose. See "Google user data" below for exactly what is accessed.
  • Challonge sign-in: you sign in to Challonge and authorize the app. The resulting access tokens are used to push and run your bracket and are stored locally in an encrypted store or OS keychain.
  • Tournament metadata: name, dates, player lists — entered by the administrator to operate the app.

How Information Is Stored

OAuth tokens and third-party credentials are stored locally on the administrator's machine (OS keychain or encrypted local store). The application does not transmit or store tokens on external servers. All tournament and player data lives only on your device.

How Information Is Used

Information you enter is used solely to:

  • Display your tournament dashboard within the app
  • Update the title and description of your Google Form with tournament details, and import your form's signups, via the Google Forms API
  • Create and update your Challonge bracket via the Challonge REST API

No information is used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app's features.

Third-Party Services

The app connects to these external services on your behalf:

  • Challonge (Fandom, Inc.) - receives tournament name, team names, player names, dates, and stream link to populate your bracket. See Fandom's Privacy Policy.
  • Google (Alphabet Inc.) - receives the form title and description the app writes to your signup form. See Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not control how these services handle data once it is transmitted to them. Your use of those services is governed by their respective privacy policies.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third party beyond the two services listed above, and only as needed to operate the features you use.

Analytics and Telemetry

The Colosseum does not include any analytics, crash reporting, or telemetry. No usage data is sent anywhere.

Data Retention and Deletion

All app data lives on your device. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app or manually removing the application data folder. Uninstalling the app removes all stored preferences and credentials.

Children's Privacy

The Colosseum is intended for use by administrators managing game tournaments. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Google user data

The Colosseum requests two Google Forms permissions (OAuth scopes). It uses them only on the single signup form you choose, and for no other purpose:

  • forms.body (see, edit, create, and delete your Google Forms): used only to read the form's question list, to find the Name and Discord questions, and to update the form's title and description with your tournament details. The app does not create or delete forms and does not edit questions.
  • forms.responses.readonly (see all responses to your Google Forms): used only to import each respondent's name, Discord handle, and submission time as tournament signups.

The app does not access any other Google data, such as Drive files, email, contacts, or calendar.

Imported signups are stored only on your device and are used only to run your tournament. Your Google refresh token is stored only on your device.

Limited Use

The Colosseum's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically, we do not use Google user data for advertising, do not sell it, do not transfer it to third parties except as needed to provide the features you use or to comply with law, and no person reads it except as you direct inside the app.

Disconnecting and revoking Google access

You can disconnect Google at any time with Disconnect in the app's Settings. This revokes the app's access at Google and deletes the token from your device. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
